The **Fibonacci sequence** is a famous mathematical pattern often found in nature—from snail shells to flower petals.

It starts with **0** and **1**. After that, every new number is just the **sum of the two numbers before it**.

### The Pattern:
- 0 + 1 = **1**
- 1 + 1 = **2**
- 1 + 2 = **3**
- 2 + 3 = **5**
- 3 + 5 = **8**
- 5 + 8 = **13**

**Your Task:** Look at the pattern above. If the last number is 13, what are the next two numbers in the sequence? Write them in the code! (Hint: 8+13=?)
