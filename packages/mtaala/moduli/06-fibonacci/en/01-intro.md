The **Fibonacci** sequence starts with **0** and **1**. Each next number is **the sum of the two numbers before it**:

```text
0, 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, ...

0 + 1 = 1
1 + 1 = 2
1 + 2 = 3
2 + 3 = 5
3 + 5 = 8
5 + 8 = 13
```

This sequence shows up all over nature: the number of petals on many flowers, the pattern of seeds in a sunflower, and the spiral of a snail's shell.

In this module you will learn three ways to compute Fibonacci numbers, and why one of them is thousands of times faster than another.
