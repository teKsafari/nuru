---
title: "Your Own Drawing"
task: "Draw anything you like: a house, a sun, a flag or a pattern. Use at least one loop and some turtle commands. At the end, print the name of your drawing with `andika()`."
---
Well done! You have learned lines, turning, loops, polygons, colour, filling, circles, stars and functions. Now it is your turn to be the artist.

Ideas to get you started:

- **House**: a square, a triangle on top as the roof, and a door.
- **Sun**: a circle filled with yellow, with rays around it.
- **Pattern**: one polygon, turn a little, repeat many times in different colours.
- **Flag**: rectangles filled with colour.

A few extra commands that may help:

```nuru
kobe.mandhari("samawati") // canvas colour
kobe.kasi(10)             // draw fast
kobe.ficha()              // hide the turtle at the end
kobe.maandishi("Jambo!")  // write words on the canvas
```

There is no single right answer here. If you like your drawing, copy your code into the **Uwanja** (playground) and press **Share** to show a friend.
