---
title: "Your First Line"
task: "Tell the turtle to walk forward `150` steps. It draws a line as it goes."
---
Meet **kobe**, the turtle! It is a small artist living in the middle of the canvas. It carries a pen on its tail: every time it walks, it draws a line.

First you bring the turtle into your program with `tumia kobe`. Then you give it commands:

```nuru
tumia kobe

kobe.+++mbele(100)+++
```

`kobe.mbele(100)` means "walk forward 100 steps". One step is one pixel on the canvas.

At the start the turtle faces **right** (east). That is why your first line goes to the right.

To make it walk backwards, use `kobe.nyuma(50)`.
