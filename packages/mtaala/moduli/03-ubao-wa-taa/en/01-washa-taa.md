title: "Switch On Your First Light"
task: "Switch on light number `1` on the board."
---
Welcome to the **light board** (`ubao`)! It is an electronics board inside your computer. It has:

- **8 lights** (LEDs) numbered 1 to 8,
- a **buzzer** (`kengele`) that makes sounds,
- a small **screen** (`skrini`) with two lines.

Real electronic devices, like traffic lights or washing machines, are controlled by programs like these.

First you bring the board into your program with `tumia ubao`. Then you give it commands:

```nuru
tumia ubao

ubao.+++washa(3)+++
```

This program switches on (`washa`) the third light. The number inside the brackets picks which light.
